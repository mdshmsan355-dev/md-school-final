# Md School — Official Result Card Design

## Approved templates

The result system uses six visual templates based on the approved reference designs:

### Children
1. children_blue — blue
2. children_green — green
3. children_orange — orange (replaces pink/purple)

### Formal
4. formal_blue — blue
5. formal_green — green
6. formal_gold — gold/bronze (replaces purple)

All six use the Yemen Republic emblem and the official header:
- الجمهورية اليمنية
- وزارة التربية والتعليم

Md School branding and developer identity are not printed on official result documents.

## Selection

A manager can choose a template independently for each:
- grade
- first term
- second term
- final result

The same selected template drives the on-screen preview and generated PDF.

## Result ranking

Ranking is dense ranking:

`1, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5`

Only ranks 1–5 are shown as top students. Students after rank 5 are not classified as top students; if they passed, their result shows **ناجح** only.

A tie at rank 5 keeps all tied students at rank 5.

## Official document content

Each card includes:
- Yemen Republic / Ministry of Education header
- school name
- result period and academic year
- student name and permanent number
- grade and section
- subject table
- maximum, student score and percentage
- total and overall percentage
- result status
- top-five rank when applicable
- school manager / class teacher / official seal spaces

The PDF is generated as A4 pages with six cards per page.
